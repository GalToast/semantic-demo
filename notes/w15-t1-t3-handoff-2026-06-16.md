## Key learnings

### The "sanctioned dead bridge" pattern

The Wave A audit correctly identified `keyboard-help-bridge.ts` and `search-results-ui-bridge.ts` as zero-consumer bridges. But they're in `tests/unit-active/svelte-bridge-import-contract.test.ts`'s `KNOWN_RETIRED_BRIDGES` allowlist as "W11-T5 Wave 1 — search-\* sanctioned passthrough" and "DOM event handlers; trivial wrapper, no Svelte port justified". They're intentionally kept for migration continuity.

**Lesson:** A "zero consumers" bridge isn't always safe to delete — the test's allowlist is the ground truth for what's intentionally retired vs. what's truly dead. The audit's recommendation needs the allowlist filter applied.
