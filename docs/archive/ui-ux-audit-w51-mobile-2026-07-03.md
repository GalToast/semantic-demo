## New mobile findings (W51-MOB-\*)

Captured at viewport 375×667, post-splash (~5.5s after first paint), `render-kind-placeholder2d`, body class `surface-idle view-galaxy navigation-journey-compass`. No `<canvas>` is rendered in this state — Placeholder2D rendered instead, expected per the AGENTS.md mobile 2D placeholder invariant.

| #   | Severity | Finding                                                                                  | Detail                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| --- | -------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| M1  | HIGH     | **🔒 emoji literal in disabled Trail/Focus/Inside mode radios**                          | From the post-load snapshot: each disabled radio exposes text content `text: 🔒` directly (the radios show as `radio "Trail" [disabled]: img img text: 🔒`). Screen readers announce "lock" (U+1F512) repeatedly when the user lands on the radiogroup. Prior desktop audit finding #10 flagged this and went unfixed; the fix is `aria-label="Trail — locked until a business is selected"` (or a SVG with `aria-hidden="true"` and the aria-label on the radio itself).                                                                                   |
| M2  | MEDIUM   | **Two `<h1>` headings visible to AT on mobile**                                          | (a) `Semantic Explorer — Montgomery County Business Network` (in `region "Application title"` placed above `<main>` per W49-G / 9e4a227d), and (b) `Semantic Explorer Preview` (in Placeholder2D's preview region, visible on mobile). Evaluate shows only (b) is `visible=true` at runtime, but both are `<h1>` in the accessibility tree — screen readers will announce two H1s on a single page. The Placeholder2D H1 should demote to `<h2>` (or the chrome H1 should demote to a visually-hidden `<p>` element with screen-reader-only `region` role). |
| M3  | MEDIUM   | **"Now explore your way." toast lingers at y≈509 even after the welcome dialog is gone** | `[role="status"]` toast 110×Px tall with text `× Now explore your way.` remains at y≈509, well within the viewport — captured 5s after the splash dismisses. Prior audit #7 flagged the dual-dialog stack (welcome + toast at once); the follow-up shows the toast doesn't auto-dismiss when the welcome dialog closes, so users see a residual cue long after they've made their first selection. Should self-dismiss when user makes a click anywhere OR after ~8s with no engagement.                                                                    |
| M4  | LOW      | **Filters pill is `position: absolute; z-index: 800`**                                   | Every other piece of mobile chrome (header chips, info panel) is `position: fixed` or static.
