## Findings

### 1. The 3D network — the entire product premise — is invisible on boot

**All three models independently: "no 3D graph/network is visible."** The hero copy
reads *"Explore Montgomery County businesses through an interactive 3D network"*
while the canvas behind it is a near-black void (one faint red/orange glow blob,
upper-right).

This is the single most damaging finding. The product advertises its marquee feature
in the headline and then does not render it.

### 2. CATEGORIES sidebar is nearly illegible

Dark charcoal panel, text in very low contrast — labels like "General Business",
"Professional Services" and counts like "2531" in dim gray. Named the **worst
offender** in the legibility scan.

### 3. `mobile-boot.png` is a black frame

2,756 bytes, confirmed by two independent passes as a uniform near-black image with no
UI. **Mobile remains completely unverified** — it is not known to be broken, only
unphotographed.

### 4. Two search inputs overlap at top-left

A duplicate ghost search field ("Search (press /)") sits behind the dark overlay.
Likely a transitional/mid-animation capture — **verify against a settled-state
screenshot before filing as a bug.**

### 5. "County" chip clipped at narrow widths

On the mobile-width capture the chip overlaps the search row and sits half-clipped at
the right edge.

### 6. CTA hierarchy — CONFLICT with source, needs resolution

- **Source read** (`src/components/Splash.svelte`): `.splash-submit` is a filled teal
  button ("Search"), `.splash-cta` is `background: transparent; text-decoration:
  underline` ("Explore") — i.e. a correct primary/secondary pair.
- **Screenshot read:** "Explore" renders as a **second solid button**, not an
  underlined link.

One of these is wrong. The screenshot is from 2026-08-14, so the most likely
explanation is that the CSS changed after the capture — but this must be settled
before acting, since it flips the recommended fix.

### 7. Brand mark

Small dark rounded square with "SE", ~24px, teal-tinted border, low contrast against
the header. Reads as "a plausible intentional logo, functional but low-contrast —
slightly washed". Not the placeholder disaster it could have been.

### 8. Hero title

White, wide-tracked, font-weight ~200, centered. Legible, but "thin/aliased — strokes
look wispy at this size; 'elegant' is the intent, 'faint' halfway wins."

### 9. Colour

~3 hues: near-black ground, teal/cyan accents, plus a faint orange/red ambient glow
upper-right that reads as a bolt-on. "Cohesive in principle… feels underdesigned
rather than clashing."

### 10. Top pill row is crowded

Seven pill buttons (Overview/Search/Trail/Focus/Inside/Map + Help), several with
dashed outlines and lock icons, all dark-on-dark — "a row of mystery buttons
screaming for attention."

## Top 5 fixes (impact per effort)

1. **Render a visible 3D network on boot** — show the graph dimmed behind the hero, or
   drop the "3D network" claim from the copy.
2. **Raise CATEGORIES sidebar contrast** to ≥ 4.5:1, or collapse the sidebar until
   search starts.
3. **Kill the duplicate overlapping top-left search input** and the clipped "County"
   chip on narrow widths (after confirming on a settled capture).
4. **Demote the top pill row** — consolidate to 3 pills max; reconsider whether locked
   modes need to be visible before a selection exists (note: this cuts against the
   standing A2-4 contract, which mandates the rail always be visible — resolve
   deliberately, do not silently revert).
5. **Settle the CTA conflict** (#6) and enforce one dominant action.

## MAJOR: CARTO basemap is unauthenticated

Every map view is flooded with diagonal **"API KEY REQUIRED —
carto.com/basemaps/apikey"** watermarks. Verified in source — hardcoded, no key, no
env var:

```
src/lib/engine/map-state.ts:118
src/lib/engine/map-state-lifecycle.ts:74   <- second site, same pattern
    L.tileLayer('https://{s}.basemaps.cartocdn.com/dark_all/{z}/{x}/{y}{r}.png', ...)
```

CARTO now watermarks unauthenticated tile requests, so to any user the map looks
broken. **Needs a decision**: supply a CARTO key, switch provider, or drop the raster
basemap. Both call sites must be changed together.

## `?anchor=<id>` deep-link intermittently hangs the renderer — PARTIALLY RETRACTED

Both `focus-1280` and `compass-360` (`?nodemo=1&anchor=519`) failed to screenshot.
An earlier version of this document called it "app logic, not GPU" and named
`src/lib/orchestration/url-restore.ts` as the prime suspect. **That conclusion was
wrong** — it was written before the measurement below.

### What is true

- It is **anchor-specific**. Control (`?nodemo=1`, no anchor): **8/8 screenshot in
  ~800 ms**. `?anchor=519`: **4/5, but 1 run never completed even at a 60 s
  timeout.**
- It is **bimodal** — either ~1 s or never. That rules out ordinary slowness.
- It is **not** the focus-pocket reactive loop. Instrumenting
  `applyLocalNeighborhoodFocus` (`src/lib/journey/focus-pocket.ts:305`) and sampling
  `window.__POCKET_COUNT` every 150 ms from load shows the counter reaching only
  **2** and stopping. No runaway.
- No JS errors, no `pageerror`, no `crash` event.
- Reproduces with `--disable-gpu`, so it is not tied to the GPU backend.

### What is NOT true (retracted)

- ~~"app logic, not GPU/environment"~~ — unresolved. The one surviving signal
  (bimodal, no JS error, CDP wedged) is equally consistent with a native-level hang
  in software-WebGL context/shader setup on the focus path, or a worker deadlock
  (mycelium-build-worker / semantic-threads), as with an app-side loop.
- ~~"`url-restore.ts` is the prime suspect"~~ — not supported by any measurement.

### Confounder found (important for anyone re-testing)

The apparent severity is **very sensitive to machine load**. Screenshots that take
~800 ms on an idle machine take ~20 s when the host is busy. Polling OS process
memory via `powershell Get-Process` every 300 ms was enough to inflate both control
and anchor screenshots from ~800 ms to ~20 s. Always re-measure on an idle machine
before concluding a hang is real.

Peak browser memory is not the cause either: control peaks ~560 MB, anchor ~755 MB,
no OOM.

### Status

Not root-caused. Next step: catch a wedged run and attach a native debugger or
capture a Chrome trace (`--enable-tracing`) to see whether the block is in JS, in
the compositor, or inside SwiftShader.

# Geometry replaces vision (2026-08-30)

## Why this section exists

Every spatial claim above came from a vision model. One of them — the ≤820px
"layer collision", ranked the #1 defect — turned out to be false. The DOM showed a
clean layout, and a z-index change made to fix it was a visual no-op (0 pixels
differ).

**Vision narratives about spatial claims on this dark, layered UI are not
trustworthy on their own.** The specific failure mode: the model reads *vertically
stacked* elements as *overlapping*. Confirm with `getBoundingClientRect` before
acting.

Findings that DID hold up were all verified independently: the CARTO basemap
(model surfaced `carto.com/basemaps/apikey`, matching source), the mode-chip
contrast (computed styles), the dead CSS (source grep), the contract test (a
passing run).

## The gate: `tests/layout-integrity-contract.mjs`

5 breakpoints (375/640/768/820/1280) x 3 surfaces (overview/map/search). Asserts
OVERFLOW, TEXT-CLIP and COLLISION from measured boxes.

```
node tests/layout-integrity-contract.mjs
```

Its `ALLOWLIST` is a ratchet: each entry is a real un-fixed defect, and fixing one
means **deleting** its entry. The gate stays green so it can run in CI, but the
list is the bug backlog.

Filters that took a full run to discover — without them the signal is ~99% noise:

| Excluded | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Leaflet tiles | A pannable map has tiles outside the viewport in every direction |
| SVG internals (`g`/`path`/`circle`) | They report geometric boxes, not layout boxes |
| Ancestor/descendant pairs | Nested boxes intersect by definition |
| "Backdrops" covering ≥90% of viewport | A full-screen splash over the map is intent — this exact pair was the false positive |
| Sub-4px clips | A 3px compass-step clip appeared at *every* breakpoint; it is line-height rounding |

## ≤820px collision — NOT CONFIRMED, strike from the list

- Not a capture artifact: 2s / 6s / 12s captures at 768px are pixel-identical.
- DOM at 768px is clean: splash-frame 512x363, title 448x48, search 416x57, Search
  button right edge 585 (< 768). No modal, no categories sidebar present.
- Hypothesised z-order inversion (splash `--z-modal` 400 vs header 800); tested by
  raising the splash to `--z-blocker` 1000 → **0 pixels differ**. Reverted, not
  committed. The splash at 400 was already above `section.map-view` at 100.

The 375px collision and the 820px status truncation WERE independently confirmed,
so the vision pass was pointing at something real — it just could not tell us
which claims were. The 768px one it ranked #1 is not reproducible.

## Stacking lesson (cost two wrong turns)

The empty-state had `z-index: 1000` vs the splash's `400` and *still* painted
underneath, because it is confined inside `.map-container` (`position: fixed;
z-index: 2` → its own stacking context). A child's z-index is scoped to its
stacking context. The visible symptom came from the splash background being
`rgba(0,0,0,0.92)` — the text beneath bled through at 8% rather than being
covered.

**Check the ancestor chain (`tmp/probe-stack.mjs`) before assuming a z-index
value is wrong.**
