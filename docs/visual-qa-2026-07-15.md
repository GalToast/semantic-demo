## 4. Findings

Severity: **P0** breaks the core product promise · **P1** significant UX defect · **P2** polish/consistency · **P3** hygiene.

### F1 · P0 — The focus pocket constellation is invisible (vision-confirmed, root-caused)

**What the user sees:** landing on `?anchor=519` shows a small bright teal ring surrounded by field density indistinguishable from everywhere else. **Six independent vision judges confirm** (full-res 1:1 crop `crop-04-focus-center.png` + mobile crop): MiniMax-M3 (_"I would not call this a readable 'pocket cluster' to a new viewer"_), mistral-large-2512 (_"NO… uniform scatter of faint dots, indistinguishable from the rest of the field"_), mistral-medium-2508 (_"NO… no meaningful visual distinction or 'focus mode' effect"_), Qwen3-VL-235B (_"NO… no distinct constellation"_), magistral-medium-2509 (_"NO… some dots slightly brighter or larger but scattered and not forming a clear group"_), and **mistral-large-3-675b** — the strongest free vision lane — (_"NO. Around the bright teal ring, I see only a sparse scattering of small, faint dots—no distinct, tight constellation of brighter or larger dots"_). Confidence: VERY HIGH.

**Root cause (two cooperating defects):**

1. **The dominant Points layer never pocket-transforms neighbor positions.** `lerpNodesForFrame` (src/lib/engine/three-engine-frame-updates.ts:205-252) lerps `state.nodePositions` toward focus targets and updates only the **spore** InstancedMesh (`setNodeSporeInstanceMatrixPort`). The `points-instanced-field` geometry `position` attribute is written once at creation (src/lib/engine/node-manager.ts:488-493) and never updated — no `position.needsUpdate` for the Points mesh exists anywhere in `src/`. Only the **anchor** moves in the Points layer (separate path); the 21 satellites stay at overview coordinates.
2. **But their colors DO change** — `applyPointFilterColors` (src/lib/journey/point-color.ts:86-115) saturates primary/support/anchor colors. Result: ~21 bright dots **scattered across the overview field**, away from the anchor. Multi-model pixel verdict: brightening is visible (MiniMax: _"isolated brighter rose and amber dots"_; magistral: _"slightly brighter or larger but scattered"_; Qwen3-VL: _"a few scattered dots show warm hues: faint amber, rose"_; mistral-large saw the brightness but no readable hue) — engine fact confirmed in-pixel; hue readability is marginal (see F11).

The spore layer (which does transform) renders at 0.65 opacity, ~2.5× smaller than the anchor scale, and is visually dominated by the Points layer.

### F2 · P0 — Anchor→neighbor connection lines are a dead feature

`webglContext.focusSemanticConnectionPairs` is declared (src/lib/engine/webgl-context.ts:43), initialized empty (:91), cleared in teardown (lifecycle.ts:551) — and **never written anywhere in `src/`** (grep-verified). The mycelium analog `myceliumConnectionPairs` has a real writer (thread-manager.ts:43-83); the focus equivalent has none. The visible teal `LineSegments` (0x4ecdc4, opacity 0.5, 342 segments) bunches every segment within ±0.001 of the anchor — a starburst blob, not rays. Vision lane: _"they read as a single teal blob at the anchor rather than 22 distinct rays."_ Meanwhile the trail toolbar lists 12 neighbor stops ("Next: Willow Creek Plaza Lp") with no rendered 3D counterpart.

### F3 · P1 — First-visit help dialog blocks deep-link targets

Vision lane flagged that the "Explore Montgomery County businesses visually" dialog sits over the focal area in `03-focus-entry`, `12-semantic-dive`, and `14-search-to-focus-handoff`. ### F7 · P1 — Semantic dive's delta is real but sits at the animation-noise floor

Engine delta on record: dive line opacities ≈ focus (0.149/0.070/0.106 vs 0.157/0.057/0.087 — absolute deltas of 0.01–0.02) and lens opacity 0.24 → 0.36. **Pixel-diff ground truth** (`15-semantic-dive-clean.png` vs `15b-focus-clean.png`, dialog dismissed, PowerShell per-pixel diff): 13.7% of pixels changed vs a same-state animation baseline of 6.5% (`06` vs `07`, 4s apart) — but strongly-changed pixels are equal (2.15% vs 2.13%), so the dive delta lives entirely in the mild band (small opacity shifts), concentrated at the lens zone (bottom-center/right cells: 33–41%). Perception check across 4 strong judges: Qwen3-VL-235B called them _"visually identical"_ (twice, PNG and JPEG); mistral-large/medium reported differences but hallucinated specifics (e.g., "sidebar absent" — FocusCard.svelte:419-439 only restyles the card in dive, never hides it). A mode toggle whose delta sits at the animation-noise floor — such that even strong VL models disagree on whether it exists — is broken as designed. Dive needs its own visual identity (aggressive background dimming + intra-neighborhood edge emphasis), not ±0.02 opacity wiggles. (First capture attempt `12-semantic-dive.png` was unjudgeable — F3's dialog occluded the lens region; recaptured clean.)
