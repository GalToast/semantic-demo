## Specific steps

### Step 1: Read Canvas.svelte

Read `src/components/Canvas.svelte` in full. Find the `<canvas>` element and its attributes.

### Step 2: Add aria-keyshortcuts

Add the `aria-keyshortcuts` attribute to the canvas element listing all supported keyboard shortcuts:

```svelte
<canvas
  bind:this={canvasEl}
  class="semantic-canvas"
  width={$viewport.width * $viewport.dpr}
  height={$viewport.height * $viewport.dpr}
  role="application"
  aria-label="3D semantic business explorer"
  aria-keyshortcuts="ArrowLeft ArrowRight ArrowUp ArrowDown Home End"
  tabindex={interactive ? 0 : -1}
></canvas>
```

**Note:** The `+` and `-` keys are also supported for zoom, but `aria-keyshortcuts` doesn't have standard names for these. You can include them as `"Equal Minus"` (their key codes) or omit them if the screen reader doesn't support them.
